# Data terms

The Finance Calendar dataset and API are free for any use, commercial included, with Finance Calendar attribution and any additional original-source terms carried by individual records.

Attribution means a visible link to https://www.financecalendar.com near where the data is shown, or on your application's about or credits page. The link text can be "Finance Calendar" or "financecalendar.com".

You may cache, store, redistribute and build on the data. Please keep the `attribution` object or the source line when you redistribute files.

Records with `source_license` retain their original attribution, licence link, adaptation information and licence notice. Preserve these when displaying or redistributing those records; the Finance Calendar attribution does not replace upstream credits. Source licences currently include CC0, Unlicense, MIT and CC BY 4.0 for selected original-source crypto material.

Earnings dates are not part of the dataset (upstream licensing). Dates marked `status: estimated` may come from a recurrence rule or a publisher calendar that explicitly marks its schedule provisional. They may move. A successful source fetch does not make a provisional date final.

No warranty. Verify anything you trade on against the official source linked in each row.
