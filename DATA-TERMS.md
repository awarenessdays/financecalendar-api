# Data terms

The Finance Calendar dataset and API are free for any use, commercial included, on one condition: attribution.

Attribution means a visible link to https://www.financecalendar.com near where the data is shown, or on your application's about or credits page. The link text can be "Finance Calendar" or "financecalendar.com".

You may cache, store, redistribute and build on the data. Please keep the `attribution` object or the source line when you redistribute files.

Earnings dates are not part of the dataset (upstream licensing). Dates marked `status: estimated` come from a rule rather than a published calendar and may move.

No warranty. Verify anything you trade on against the official source linked in each row.

Contact: https://www.financecalendar.com/about/
