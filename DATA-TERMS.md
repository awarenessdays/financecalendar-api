# Data terms

Access to the complete current Finance Calendar dataset download requires an active Scale subscription. Public API documentation, developer source code and bounded API responses retain their applicable access and allowance rules; see the [current plans](https://www.financecalendar.com/api/#plans). Full snapshots and historical capture files are not included in this public source repository.

Current account and subscription terms: [API service terms](https://www.financecalendar.com/api/terms/).

This access requirement does not revoke previously granted reuse permissions for lawfully obtained copies or change upstream source licences. Commercial reuse remains subject to Finance Calendar attribution and all additional source-specific terms carried by individual records.

Attribution means a visible link to https://www.financecalendar.com near where the data is shown, or on your application's about or credits page. The link text can be "Finance Calendar" or "financecalendar.com".

You may cache, store, redistribute and build on the data subject to the applicable source terms carried by each record. Please keep the `attribution` object or the source line when you redistribute files.

Records with `source_license` retain their original attribution, `attribution_url`, licence link, adaptation information and licence notice. Preserve these when displaying or redistributing those records; the Finance Calendar attribution does not replace upstream credits. Source licences currently include CC0, Unlicense, MIT and CC BY 4.0 for selected crypto material. Licensed PatternPlus records require the linked credit [Data: PatternPlus](https://pattern.plus); retain its CC BY 4.0 link and adaptation notice.

`source_rights` identifies independently normalised facts and their source/access basis. It does not assert a publisher copyright licence. Preserve those factual-source notices and evidence links; a Finance Calendar software licence does not relicense upstream data. `discovery_url` is a discovery reference, separate from primary verification evidence.

Earnings dates are not part of the dataset (upstream licensing). Dates marked `status: estimated` may come from a recurrence rule or a publisher calendar that explicitly marks its schedule provisional. They may move. A successful source fetch does not make a provisional date final.

No warranty. Verify anything you trade on against the official source linked in each row.

Contact: https://www.financecalendar.com/about/

Historical archive rows retain their reviewed official-source notices, including
BLS public-domain material, ONS Open Government Licence, ECB/Eurostat reuse
conditions, the Statistics Canada Open Licence and ABS CC BY 4.0 where applicable.
Preserve each record's exact source attribution, unit and transformation evidence;
the software MIT licence does not replace those source conditions. Current-vintage
observations and FC evidence receipt bounds do not establish original prints,
historical consensus or past market availability. See the [history guide](docs/history.md)
and authenticated coverage for the actual scope.

The new `/history/observations`, `/history/releases` and `/history/coverage`
archive follows the active plan window: Starter one year, Build ten years,
Scale all available history. Full pagination is available within the authorized
range; all-available history is not a new bulk ZIP product. Existing `/history`,
curated data and explicit prior licences keep their applicable rights. The
complete-current-snapshot Scale download remains a separate feature.
